## 4. MATHEMATICAL EXPLANATION

### Why Minutes Dominate:

- **Minutes importance**: 0.1544 (100% relative weight)
- **Form importance**: ~0.0000 to -0.0020 (0-1.3% relative weight)
- **Ratio**: Minutes is ~77x to ∞x more important than form!

### The Math:

Prediction ≈ Σ(feature_value × feature_importance)

For a player with:
- Minutes: 90 → Contribution ≈ 90 × 0.1544 = 13.896
- Form: 5.0 → Contribution ≈ 5.0 × 0.0000 = 0.000

Even if Form = 10.0:
- Form contribution ≈ 10.0 × 0.0000 = 0.000

**Conclusion**: A player playing 90 minutes contributes ~14 points to their prediction
regardless of their form, while form contributes almost nothing.

## 5. WHY IS FORM SO LOW?

From the permutation importance analysis:

1. **Form is redundant** with other features:
   - total_points (importance: 0.0166) already captures recent performance
   - Minutes (0.1544) is a stronger predictor of future points

2. **Form has negative importance** (-0.0020 for CTX_form):
   - This suggests form may add noise or be misleading
   - The model learned that high form doesn't reliably predict future points
   - Players in 'hot streaks' often regress to the mean

3. **Model prefers underlying stats**:
   - Creativity (0.0066), Influence (0.0051), Threat (0.0022)
   - These are more stable predictors than form
